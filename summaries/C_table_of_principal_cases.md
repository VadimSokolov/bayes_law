This appendix is a table of the cases discussed in the text, arranged by jurisdiction in three groups: England and Wales, the United Kingdom and the Privy Council; the United States; and France, Italy, India, Kenya, Malawi and the European Court of Human Rights. Each entry gives the case name and year or neutral citation, the point for which the book cites it, and the chapter where it is principally discussed. The entries range over evidence and probability, tort and causation, loss of a chance, misrepresentation and insurance, contract damages, penalty clauses and frustration, pleading standards, class actions, securities fraud, jury selection and discrimination, expert evidence, and the use of artificial intelligence by lawyers. Examples are Donoghue v Stevenson, Barnett v Chelsea and Kensington HMC, Bell Atlantic v Twombly, Ashcroft v Iqbal, Batson v Kentucky and Castaneda v Partida. A reader uses it to find where a case is treated and what it is cited for, and is told to consult the reports for the full facts and reasoning.
